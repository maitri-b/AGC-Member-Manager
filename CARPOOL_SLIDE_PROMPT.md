# Prompt for Google LLM: Create Carpool Feature Presentation Slides

## Context
Please create a presentation with at least 5 slides to explain the Carpool feature for our rally event "🎉-งานแรลลี่-10th-anniversary-agents-club-2026". The presentation should help participants understand how to plan their transportation and register their carpools for the event.

## Event Background
This is a rally event where members from different companies need to travel together. The event requires transportation planning to manage the number of cars and coordinate who travels with whom. Each participant has a unique Registration ID (รหัสการลงทะเบียน) that is used to identify them and their booking.

## Presentation Objective
Create slides that explain:
1. The purpose of the Carpool feature for the rally event
2. Why transportation planning is important
3. What is a Registration ID and how to find it
4. How to create and manage a Carpool
5. How to invite others or join existing Carpools

## Key Concepts to Cover

### 1. Registration ID (รหัสการลงทะเบียน)
- **What it is**: A unique identifier for each booking/registration
- **Format**: Text ID like "ABC-123-XYZ"
- **Where to find it**:
  - In confirmation email after registration
  - On "My Registrations" page (หน้าการลงทะเบียนของฉัน)
  - In LINE notification messages
- **Important**: People from the same company may have **different Registration IDs** if they registered separately
- **Example**: Company A might have 2 registrations:
  - Registration 1: "REG-001" (3 people: Alice, Bob, Carol)
  - Registration 2: "REG-002" (2 people: Dave, Eve)

### 2. Carpool Feature Overview
- **Purpose**: Organize transportation and manage car capacity for the rally
- **Benefits**:
  - Know exactly how many cars are coming
  - Plan parking and rally checkpoints
  - Help members coordinate rides
  - Reduce environmental impact

### 3. Carpool Roles
- **Carpool Owner**: The person who creates the Carpool (registers their car)
- **Members**: People who join the Carpool (travel in that car)
- **Important Rule**: Members from the **same Registration ID** can be added directly
- **Cross-Registration**: Members from **different Registration IDs** need to be invited using their Registration ID

### 4. How to Create a Carpool (สร้าง Carpool)
**Steps:**
1. Go to the event detail page
2. Find the "Carpool" section
3. Click "Create Carpool" (สร้าง Carpool)
4. Enter your car's license plate (เช่น กท 1234)
5. Select members from your registration to join this car
6. Save

**Example Scenario:**
- Registration ID: "REG-001"
- Registered people: Alice, Bob, Carol (3 people)
- Alice creates a Carpool with license plate "กท 1234"
- Alice selects: Alice, Bob (2 people) to join this car
- Carol will join a different car or create her own

### 5. How to Invite Members from Other Registrations (เชิญสมาชิกจากบริษัทอื่น)
**When to use**: When you want to invite someone from a **different Registration ID** to join your Carpool

**Steps:**
1. Open your Carpool
2. Click "Add Members" (เพิ่มสมาชิก)
3. Search for the other person's Registration ID
4. Select which members from that registration to invite
5. Confirm

**Example Scenario:**
- Your Carpool: License "กท 1234", currently has Alice, Bob from "REG-001"
- You want to invite Dave from "REG-002"
- Search for "REG-002"
- Select Dave
- Now your car has: Alice, Bob, Dave (3 people from 2 different registrations)

### 6. How to Join an Existing Carpool (ขอเข้าร่วม Carpool)
**Option A: Contact the car owner directly**
- Ask them to invite you using your Registration ID
- They will search for your Registration ID and add you

**Option B: Admin assistance**
- Contact the event admin
- Provide your Registration ID and the car you want to join
- Admin will help add you to the Carpool

### 7. Important Notes
- Each person can only be in **ONE** Carpool at a time
- You need to know your **Registration ID** to participate
- The car owner can manage members (add/remove)
- Admin can see all Carpools and assign car numbers for the rally
